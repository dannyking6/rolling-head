# Rolling Head

## About the game

Roll over hills, walls and checkered platforms as a bobbling head with one hilarious superpower: a stretchy, rubbery neck that snaps you forward like a slingshot. Every level is a bite-sized obstacle course of ramps, glass panes, spinning hazards and bouncing pads where one perfect fling sends your head flying past rival racers to the finish line.

The magic is in the feel — the squash-and-stretch of every launch, the satisfying gulp when your head swallows a coin trail, the confetti blast when you claim first place. Short, juicy runs make it impossible to play just one more level, and unlockable head skins keep the next run tempting. Pick up and play in seconds, chase the perfect fling for hours.

## Controls

### Smartphone & tablet

- Touch and hold anywhere, then drag: stretch the elastic neck in the direction you want to fling.
- Release: launch! The further you drag, the bigger the boost.
- While rolling, tap to nudge the head downward and hug the track on ramps and drops.

### Desktop

- Click and hold with the left mouse button, drag to aim the stretchy neck, release to fling.
- While rolling, left-click to press the head down for better traction.
> Trackpad: single click = same as left-click; click-and-drag works exactly like touch-and-drag.
